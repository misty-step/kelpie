A — Workflow first

Tall draft hero is the primary task; a pending ask reuses that exact footprint as a stacked decision card.

Tradeoffs
  • Gains: Draft intent unmistakable; ask cannot be ignored; state switcher makes comparison fast.
  • Costs: Tall hero uses vertical space; rail disappears during ask.
  • Risks: Long drafts compress transcript; Other needs production submit parity.
  • Rejected: thin banner over live draft; labelled Send; + button; permanent Enter hint.
